

## Braj Ras 2026 — Landing Page Plan

### Design Direction
Rich & royal aesthetic — deep purples, maroons, and gold accents. Decorative serif/script fonts reminiscent of the logo (similar to a calligraphic Indian style). Dark backgrounds with warm golden highlights. Elegant dividers and ornamental elements between sections.

### Sections (top to bottom)

1. **Hero** — Full-viewport, dark cinematic background with gradient overlay. "BrajRas" in large decorative script font, "2026" below, tagline "The Mellows of Śrī Vṛndāvan Dhām." Subtle animated shimmer on the title. Scroll-down indicator.

2. **Video Section** — Centered video placeholder with ornate gold border frame. Play button overlay. Caption below.

3. **7 Deities of Śrī Vṛndāvana** — Horizontal scrolling cards or staggered grid. Each deity gets a card with a gold-bordered icon placeholder, name in decorative font, and the one-line description. Alternating maroon/purple tones.

4. **Corners of Vraj** — Full-width section with a parallax-style background. Four feature blocks in a 2×2 grid: Govardhan, Hidden ghats, Local flavors, Parikrama paths. Each with an icon and short evocative text.

5. **Where Līlā Still Echoes** — Atmospheric section with dark forest-themed background. Kamyavan description prominently featured. Three smaller items below: sacred kundas, fields, childhood places. Storytelling layout with offset text and image placeholders.

6. **VCM Facilities** — Six-card bento grid layout: Temple Hall, Parks, Festival, Prasadam, Stay, Yamuna Vihar. Each card has an icon, title, and short description. Cards have gold border accents on hover.

7. **Trip Perks** — Horizontal icon strip with key perks: Soul-stirring kirtans, Discover Vraj, curated experiences. Clean, minimal section with gold icon accents.

8. **Testimonials** — "Previous Experiences" section. Carousel of testimonial cards with quotes, names, and subtle decorative quotation marks. Placeholder content for now.

9. **Registration CTA** — Full-width section with gradient background. Heading: "Begin Your Journey." Form fields: Name, Email, Phone, Number of attendees. Submit button with gold styling. Form validates with zod.

10. **Footer** — Minimal footer with BrajRas logo, social links, and contact info.

### Typography
- Google Font **"Cinzel Decorative"** for headings (royal, serif feel similar to the logo)
- **"Cormorant Garamond"** for body text (elegant serif)

### Interactions
- Smooth scroll navigation
- Fade-in animations on scroll for each section
- Hover effects on cards with subtle gold glow
- Sticky minimal navbar with section links
